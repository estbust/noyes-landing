Conn. Gen. Stat. § 52-584 • Mandatory Filing Deadline

Connecticut 2-Year Statute of Limitations Notice

In Connecticut, accident injury victims strictly have two (2) years from the date of injury to commence formal legal proceedings under Conn. Gen. Stat. § 52-584. If your deadline passes without formal filing, your legal right to financial recovery is permanently barred. Under Connecticut's modified comparative fault law (§ 52-572h), you can recover damages as long as you are 50% or less at fault. Moreover, immigration status is legally protected and cannot be used by insurers to reduce damages.
